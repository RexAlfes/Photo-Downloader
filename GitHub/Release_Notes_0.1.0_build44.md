## Photo Downloader 0.1.0 – Alpha build 44

> **Alpha software.** Keep your original files until you have checked the copies. Windows is the primary platform.

### What's new
- **List view column headings.** The list view has a heading line (File, Captured, Status, Camera, Destination). Click a heading to sort by it, click again to reverse; an arrow shows the sorted column. The thumbnail views and the viewer follow the same order. Downloads and sequence numbers still follow card order.
- Build 43 changes carry over: safe delete (Recycle Bin or a hidden trash folder on the card) and auto-rotate of progressive JPEGs.

### In development
Video and audio files.

### Known limitations
- Card-reader detection, eject and single-instance handling are Windows only.
- Not yet implemented: USB camera download (PTP/MTP), GPS geotagging, watch mode, download history.
- Safe delete, progressive rotation and the sortable headings are new and need testing.
